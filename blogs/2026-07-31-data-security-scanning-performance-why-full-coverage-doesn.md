Key takeaways The real limit on scan speed is usually API rate limits set by the cloud provider being scanned. Dynamic Data Concentration and Smart Scan work together to cut redundant scanning and surface high-risk data first. Automated remediation is what actually closes exposure at scale — in one environment, 3 million overexposed records dropped to 2,000 in two days.
