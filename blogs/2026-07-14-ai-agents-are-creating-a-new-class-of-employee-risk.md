Varonis security brief AI agents are creating a new digital workforce, rapidly increasing the number of non-human identities accessing sensitive data. As AI becomes more autonomous, security shifts from controlling access to controlling actions and intent. Organizations need a data-centric security layer that enforces policy, reduces risk, and enables safe AI adoption.
