AI is being deployed everywhere, fast. Copilots summarize meetings. Code assistants write production software. Sales agents prospect and qualify leads. Customer service bots resolve tickets. And increasingly, fully autonomous agents read, write, and act on enterprise data without a human in the loop. The speed of adoption has outpaced security’s ability to keep up. Only about 3% of the average organization’s data estate is connected to AI today, and analysis of enterprise environments consistently shows that 99% of organizations have sensitive data exposed to AI tools.
